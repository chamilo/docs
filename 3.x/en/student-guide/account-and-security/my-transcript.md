# My Transcript

If your platform has enabled academic transcripts, you can view a record of your completed, certified courses and download an unofficial transcript. Your institution may also issue signed official transcripts.

## Open Your Transcript

1. Sign in and open **My transcript** from the top navigation (when this option is available).
2. Or visit `/transcript` on your Chamilo portal while signed in.
3. Review your completed courses, course codes, grades, dates, hours, overall grade and GPA.
4. Select **Download unofficial transcript** to save a PDF of the current record.

The record is built from eligible certificates generated by the root assessment (gradebook) of courses and sessions in which you are still enrolled. A course may be missing if it has not produced a qualifying certificate. If no courses qualify, Chamilo displays **No completed course yet.**

The grade letters and grade-point average depend on your institution's configured grading scale. GPA is calculated as an unweighted average of the grade points of the listed courses, unless your administrator changes how the underlying courses and grades are recorded.

## Official Transcripts

Under **Official transcripts**, you can find signed versions issued for you by authorized staff, when any exist. Each version records the issuing date and issuer. Use **Download** for its PDF and **Verify** to check the digital signature on its verification page.

An official version is a snapshot of your record when it was issued. It is **not** automatically updated when later grades or courses change; your institution can issue a newer version. Authorized staff may revoke an older version, and its public verification page will then show that revocation.

You cannot issue or revoke official transcripts yourself. Ask your institution if you require a new or corrected signed transcript. Share public verification links thoughtfully: someone with the link may be able to see details of a valid signed transcript.

If **My transcript** is missing, this feature may not be enabled on your portal. For administrative setup, see [Academic Transcripts](../../admin-guide/users/academic-transcripts.md).
