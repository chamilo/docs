# Following a Learning Path

A **learning path** guides you through a structured sequence of activities — documents, tests, links, and more — in an order your teacher has defined. On many courses, it's the main (or only) tool you'll actually need to use, since it can bundle everything else inside it.

## Opening a Learning Path

Open the **Learning paths** <img src="/.gitbook/assets/icons/mdi-map-marker-path.svg" alt="Learning paths" data-size="line"> tool from the course homepage and click on one to start. If your teacher enabled **auto-launch**, the learning path may open automatically as soon as you enter the course.

![The learning paths list for a course, each showing its own progress](/.gitbook/assets/student-learning-paths-list.png)

## Moving Through the Path

Inside a learning path, you'll see:

* A **list of items** (steps) in a panel, organized into sections your teacher may have grouped like chapters
* The **content of the current item** in the main area — a document, an embedded test, a link, and so on

As you complete items, they're marked with a checkmark, and a **progress bar** shows your overall completion percentage for the whole path. If you leave and come back later, you resume exactly where you left off — nothing is lost.

## Locked Items and Prerequisites

Some items may appear **locked** until you meet a prerequisite your teacher set — typically finishing an earlier item, or reaching a minimum score on a test earlier in the path. If you can't open a step, check whether an earlier one is still incomplete.

## Tests and Certificates Inside a Path

A learning path can include a test as one of its steps — taking it works the same as described in [Taking Tests](taking-tests.md). Some paths end with a **certificate** step: reaching it (having met any score requirements along the way) triggers your completion certificate, if your teacher has configured one for the course.

## SCORM and Other Imported Content

Some learning path items aren't built directly in Chamilo, but imported as a **SCORM** package, a **CMI5/xAPI** package, or built with the C-Studio plugin. These generally look and behave like any other interactive content — Chamilo tracks your progress and score through them the same way, even though the content itself was authored elsewhere.

## Tips

* **Use the progress bar** to gauge how much of the path is left.
* **Don't worry about losing your place** — Chamilo remembers exactly where you are in a path between sessions.
* **A locked step usually means unfinished work earlier in the path** — go back and check rather than assuming access was denied by mistake.
