# Glossary

Key terms used throughout this guide.

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Assessment** | See **Gradebook** below |
| **Assignment** | A task where you submit files or text for your teacher to review and grade. Also known as "student publication" or "work". |
| **CAPTCHA** | A challenge (typically typing distorted letters from an image) used to confirm you're a real person, shown on some login, registration, and lost-password forms. |
| **Catalog** | The public listing of courses (and sessions) that you can browse and, in some cases, self-enroll into. Accessed via **Explore more courses**. |
| **Certificate** | A document automatically generated when you meet the minimum score defined in a course's gradebook. |
| **Course** | A container for learning content and tools. A course can include documents, exercises, forums, learning paths, and more. |
| **Enrollment** | Being registered in a course or session, giving you access to its content. Also known as "subscription". |
| **Exercise** | A quiz or test containing one or more questions. Also referred to as a "test" in some contexts. |
| **Forum** | A discussion tool with threaded conversations organized into categories and topics. |
| **Gradebook** | A tool that aggregates your scores from exercises, assignments, and other activities into a unified grade. |
| **Invitation** | A one-time link sent by a teacher to your e-mail address, letting you register and get automatically subscribed to a course, even if general registration is closed. |
| **Learning path** | A structured sequence of learning activities (documents, exercises, links) you follow in a defined order, sometimes with prerequisites. |
| **Self-registration** | The ability to create your own account through a public registration form, if enabled by the platform administrator. |
| **Session** | A time-bound edition of one or more courses assigned to a specific group of learners, such as a term or cohort. |
| **Skill** | A competency linked to gradebook achievements. Earning a certificate can validate associated skills. |
| **Two-factor authentication (2FA)** | An extra sign-in step using a time-based code from an authenticator app, in addition to your password. |
| **Visibility** | A setting that controls whether a tool, document, or activity is shown to learners. Teachers can always see hidden items. |
